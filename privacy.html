Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Ripple is built to keep your music on your Mac. This page explains exactly what the Ripple app and this website do with data, and what goes to the few services Ripple relies on.

Audio stays on your Mac

To animate the Blob and Glass bars modes, Ripple analyses the sound of the music app you choose (Spotify or Apple Music) in real time, on your Mac. Audio is never recorded, saved or uploaded, and Ripple does not use your microphone. macOS asks for your permission (Screen & System Audio Recording) before this works; Ripple uses it only for your music app's audio and does not read or save screen images.

What's playing

With your permission (macOS Automation), Ripple reads the current song's title, artist, album, artwork and playback position from the Spotify or Music app on your Mac, and sends playback commands when you use its controls. This stays on your Mac, except for lyrics.

Lyrics

To show timed lyrics, Ripple sends the song's title, artist, album and length to LRCLIB, a free public lyrics service, and receives the lyrics back. Like any web service, LRCLIB also sees your IP address. No account or personal identifier is sent.

Spotify account (optional)

If you choose to connect your Spotify account, it's used only for the heart button (adding or removing a song from your Liked Songs). Sign-in happens on Spotify's own page; Ripple stores the resulting access tokens in your Mac's Keychain and talks directly to Spotify. Spotify's own privacy policy applies to your Spotify account.

Licenses and purchases

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which sells Ripple as our reseller (merchant of record) and processes your payment, billing and tax details under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address, country and order details from Lemon Squeezy so we can provide support and refunds. We never see your full card details.

To check that a subscription is active, the Ripple app sends your license key and this Mac's name (for example "Hassaan's MacBook Pro", so you can tell your Macs apart) to Lemon Squeezy when you activate it, about once a day afterwards, and when you deactivate it. Ripple stores the key and check times in your Mac's Keychain.

The 7-day free trial is tracked only on your Mac, in the Keychain. Starting it sends nothing anywhere.

Diagnostic reports

Ripple can create a problem report when you ask it to. You see it and save it yourself; it is never sent automatically and contains no song, lyrics, audio, screenshots, account details, tokens or file paths.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, tracking or advertising, and loads nothing from other companies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Legal basis and retention

We process purchase and license data to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and keep invoices for as long as Austrian tax law requires. Support emails are kept while needed to help you and then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, restriction of or objection to its processing, and a copy of it, by emailing [[OWNER-EMAIL]]. You can also complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (dsb.gv.at).
